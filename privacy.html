Dragonwake Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

This policy applies to the Dragonwake Android app published by Mr Crops and distributed through Google Play.

Gameplay reports

Dragonwake sends structured gameplay reports to Dragonwake Discord logs when an adventure starts, when certain achievements or major events occur, and when an adventure ends. We use these reports to understand game balance, progression, and problems encountered during play.

Reports can include the app version, game mode, difficulty, character race and class, game statistics, abilities, achievements, defeated monsters, general in-game location, elapsed play time, step count, and a game-generated cause of death. Reports do not include your character name, user-entered text, Android ID, advertising ID, installation ID, device model, account information, contact information, or save files. Dragonwake does not assign a persistent identifier to connect reports from the same Android installation.

Gameplay reporting is enabled by default. You can disable it at any time under Options > Share Run Reports without affecting the game. Reports are transmitted over HTTPS and may remain in the Discord logs until they are no longer useful and are manually deleted. Discord processes the request under its privacy policy, including technical connection information such as the source IP address.

Other data practices

Dragonwake does not include advertising or account creation. We do not sell gameplay reports or use them for advertising.

Data stored on your device

Dragonwake stores save games, settings, and other gameplay state locally on your device. Only the limited gameplay fields described above are used in gameplay reports; save files are not transmitted. You can remove individual saves from the load-game screen. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data according to Android's normal behavior.

Google Play

Google Play processes purchases, downloads, and related platform activity under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Dragonwake does not receive your payment-card details.

Support email

If you email us for support, you voluntarily provide your email address and the information in your message. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support, legal, or security purposes.

Children

Dragonwake is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Dragonwake's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when that happens.
